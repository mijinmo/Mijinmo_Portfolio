## Hello

I’m Sihao Chen, also known as Paul. I’m currently a Level Designer on Arknights at Hypergryph, contributing to a range of level, character, and gameplay designs.

I studied Biomedical Science at the Zhejiang University–University of Edinburgh Institute. Out of sheer interest and curiosity, I chose to focus on systems neuroscience, which gave me the opportunity to take part in neuroscience research and data analysis.

Once I joined a lab, though, I found that neuroscience research was quite different from what I had imagined. Things didn’t go smoothly—I seemed to hit a wall at every turn. In my final undergraduate year, I decided to change direction and try making something fun, like video games.

I started making small games in 2022, including Exp10sion, which was released in 2023. I later studied game design at UC Santa Cruz, where I made the yo-yo game Kwyjibo Adventure as my capstone project.

In independent game projects, I often work on both design and programming: from levels built around time puzzles to the main design of yo-yo combat and various interaction prototypes. This website is a record of my work and creative projects, including my explorations as a student.

In my spare time, I’m also slowly working on a few games just for fun, including Project Insectoid and Project Sheep. When I have demos ready, I’ll share them here. You’re welcome to give them a try!

And although I don’t have much time for film photography these days, I’ve kept a few of my favorite shots on this site. I actually post more photos on Gamecores, so feel free to take a look if you’re interested!
