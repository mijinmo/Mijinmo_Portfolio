## From research to games

Hi, I’m Sihao Chen. You can also call me mijinmo or Paul. I’m a Level Designer on Arknights at Hypergryph, contributing to level, character, and gameplay design.

My undergraduate background is in Biomedical Science at the Zhejiang University–University of Edinburgh Institute, where I worked on neuroscience and data analysis. I later studied game design at UC Santa Cruz and created the yo-yo capstone project Kwyjibo Adventure.

In independent projects, I often work across design and programming: from time-puzzle levels to the main design of a yo-yo combat game and experimental interaction prototypes. This site brings together my professional work and creative practice, including student explorations.
